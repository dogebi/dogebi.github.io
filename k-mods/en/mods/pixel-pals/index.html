pixel-pals

pixel-pals · by hoobnn · plaxagoras

Pixel pals cross the band above the prompt while Claude works: Nyan Cat, Clawd, a shoot-em-up and more. Six curated scenes plus clawd-tales' Clawd story, in Korean

❯ /plugin marketplace add SeongGwangJu/k-mods
❯ /plugin install pixel-pals@k-mods
$ claude plugin marketplace add SeongGwangJu/k-mods
$ claude plugin install pixel-pals@k-mods

Run /reload-plugins to apply it to an open session. Needs Claude Code 2.1.287 or later.

Notes

  • Two originals merged: the scenes and pet are hoobnn's spinner (hoobnn/hoobnn-agent-mods @ 8fb6f67), the tales theme is plaxagoras' clawd-tales (@ 89c7f95). Both MIT; their copyright notices stay as-is in LICENSE, LICENSE-clawd-tales and NOTICE.
  • Of spinner's 15 themes only nyan, clawd, thunder, chomp, sparky and bluecat stay, and clawd-tales joins as a seventh theme, tales. random picks one of the seven each turn. The audio theme that read the system's sound is gone, so it runs no programs (swiftc).
  • Korean by default: /pals and /tales replies, the finale and the Clawd story's captions (the story speaks Korean or English only).
  • Two commands: /pals (themes, pet, off) and /tales (the story's demo, hats, calm mode). Preview tales with /tales demo.
  • nyan, chomp, sparky and bluecat are what the original author calls pixel homages, and Clawd is Anthropic's mascot. An unofficial fan work.
  • The scenes draw in the terminal only. In the desktop app the band shows only with the tales theme.
